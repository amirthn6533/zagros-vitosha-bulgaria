2. Scope of Commercial Activity

Zagros Vitosha Bulgaria EOOD operates as an international commodities and polymer supply specialist. The company sources high-quality prime virgin polymers (Polyethylene HDPE/LDPE, Polypropylene PP, Suspension PVC Resin S-PVC K67, Polystyrene GPPS/HIPS), asphalt Bitumen 60/70, and industrial hardware from verified global and regional petrochemical manufacturers and facilitates delivery to industrial converters in Bulgaria and neighboring European markets.

3. Commercial Terms & Incoterms 2020

All international quotations and sales contracts issued by Zagros Vitosha Bulgaria EOOD are subject to the ICC Incoterms® 2020:

  • Delivery Terms: CIF Port of Varna / Port of Burgas / Port of Mersin / Port of Istanbul or DAP to client facility yard.
  • Payment Instruments: Confirmed, Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) opened through prime international banks; Cash Against Documents (CAD); or agreed commercial T/T milestones.
  • Inspection & Quality Verification: Manufacturer batch-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA) provided with shipping documents. Third-party independent inspection by SGS, Intertek, or Cotecna at loading port is available upon buyer's request and prior agreement.
  • Ocean Demurrage Terms: Standard maritime contracts specify 14 to 21 free calendar demurrage days at destination port on container Bills of Lading to facilitate customs clearance.
