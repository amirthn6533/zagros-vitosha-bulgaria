Global Container Shipping Corridors

Discharge Ports & Maritime Logistics Infrastructure

Zagros Vitosha Bulgaria guarantees insured ocean transit (CIF / CFR Incoterms 2020) directly to key Black Sea and Mediterranean ports, backed by 14 to 21 free demurrage days and reliable bonded transit to Bulgarian factories.

Primary Bulgaria Gateway

Port of Varna (Varna-West / East)

Varna-West terminal is Bulgaria's deepest modern container hub with direct rail and highway links. Polymer containers discharged at Varna can be cleared quickly through customs and trucked to factories across Sofia, Plovdiv, Ruse, and Veliko Tarnovo within 24 hours of gate-out.

Regular Shipping Lines: MSC, Maersk, Arkas, Cosco
Demurrage Free Time: 14 to 21 Calendar Days Included
Final Delivery Options: CIF Port of Varna or DAP Factory Yard
Southern Bulgaria Gateway

Port of Burgas (BMF Port Burgas)

Strategically situated to feed the industrial manufacturing belt of Thrace, Asenovgrad, Plovdiv and Stara Zagora. Ideal for large polymer converters with fast bonded warehousing options and swift container haulage.

Terminal Facility: BMF Port Burgas (Advance Container Terminal)
Demurrage Free Time: 14 to 21 Calendar Days Included
Road Connectivity: Trakia Highway (A1) Direct to Sofia
Eastern Mediterranean Hub

Port of Mersin (MIP)

Mersin International Port provides superior container handling frequency with established bonded free-zone storage. Optimal for petrochemical supply chains and rapid re-export into regional Mediterranean markets.

Storage Facilities: Mersin Free Zone (Bonded Warehousing)
Incoterms Terms: CIF Mersin Port (MIP)
Transit Advantages: High frequency feedering with minimal port congestion
Marmara Industrial Gateway

Istanbul (Ambarlı Port Complex)

Terminals: Kumport, Marport, Mardas
