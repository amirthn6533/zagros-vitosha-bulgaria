About Zagros Vitosha Bulgaria EOOD

A dependable international commodity and polymer supply partner connecting primary petrochemical production facilities to plastics converters and industrial manufacturing operations across Bulgaria, Southeastern Europe, and Turkey.

Our Foundation

Bridging Production Hubs to European Converters

Zagros Vitosha Bulgaria EOOD was established to provide direct, transparent, and dependable access to prime petrochemical raw materials without unnecessary broker markups or delivery uncertainties.

Named after the enduring majesty of the mountain peaks, our corporation embodies stability, unwavering contract execution, and long-term supply partnerships. We specialize in bulk ocean shipments of prime Polyethylene (HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE), Polypropylene (PP Raffia & Injection), Suspension PVC Resin (S-PVC K67), Polystyrene (GPPS / HIPS), Bitumen 60/70, and industrial hardware.

By maintaining direct dispatch lines to the Port of Varna, Port of Burgas, Port of Mersin, and Port of Istanbul, we minimize logistics turnaround and provide complete trade finance flexibility, including Documentary Letters of Credit (L/C at sight), Cash Against Documents (CAD), and insured maritime CFR/CIF contracts.
