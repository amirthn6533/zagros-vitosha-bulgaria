Technical Safeguard Standards

Export Ocean Container Packaging Checklist

Zero Moisture & Shift Protocol
1. Multi-Ply Valve Bags

25 kg heavy-duty virgin PE/composite valve bags with integrated moisture barrier (WVTR < 0.5 g/m²/24h). Hermetically sealed against dust and humidity.

2. Fumigated Euro-Pallets

Heat-treated wooden pallets compliant with ISPM-15. Standardized 55 bags per pallet (1,375 kg net weight), cross-stacked for maximum stability.

3. Automated Stretch Hooding

Full waterproof elastic stretch-hood sleeve with 12-month UV stabilization. Protects pellets during open-air port staging and heavy rainfall.

4. Dunnage Air Bags & Lashing

Heavy-duty kraft dunnage air bags placed between container rows plus certified composite polyester lashing to eliminate cargo movement at sea.
